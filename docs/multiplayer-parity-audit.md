
# Multiplayer parity audit

Target: the server resolves authoritative outcomes and broadcasts campaign state/events; every browser uses the existing campaign presentation and UI. Multiplayer must not own reduced gameplay content or a second renderer.

## Verified shared or repaired

- Campaign renderer and HUD are used by multiplayer; the duplicate network HUD is hidden.
- Multiplayer enters the normal `play`/`pause` presentation states. The local campaign simulation is explicitly disabled while a network authority is active.
- FPS mouse-look, pointer lock, pause, Settings, shop/inventory/forge/special-room panels can therefore use their normal handlers.
- Player laser commands use the same canonical pointer/FPS-yaw conversion.
- Room dimensions are the shared 900×700 geometry.
- Authored templates, pillars, chambers, decorations, destructibles and static hazards are generated into authoritative room state. Client-side room fabrication was deleted.
- Authority movement and client prediction use the same obstacle collision operation.
- Lava and explosive-trap state/damage are authority-owned.
- Move and weapon catalogs are complete in shared content (47 campaign moves and 15 campaign weapons in the browser audit).
- Forge commits, inventory equip/reorder commands, shop purchases and special-room choices have shared command resolvers.
- Pickup/shop events now invoke the normal campaign item/move/weapon notifications.
- The canonical 68-relic and 6-scroll definitions are loaded once by both browser UI and authority code; the former 916-line browser copy was removed.
- Campaign item-stat derivation is shared by local play and authority. Movement, XP, coin/potion pickup, duplication, attack speed, outgoing damage, damage reduction, flat reduction and Iron Lung now consume that shared result.
- Removed a network export that overwrote the canonical item-collection transaction in the browser. This collision was why inventory increased without the normal item notification in both modes.
- Timed tool state now produces authority pulses for missiles, regeneration, lightning, mines, cape concealment and vacuum pickup radius; panic, sparkle, heal and shield activations mutate authority state. Mateo's Bag routes its equipment slot through the authoritative stored-potion/rival-sharing action, and Charged Adapter shares its ready-gate validation, charge spend, delayed walk-in portal and half-coin ladder warp transaction too.
- Shop stock, prices, item/move/weapon acquisition, next-tier trades, healing and stored potions now resolve through one shared campaign operation. Browser-only item/move/weapon stock generators and direct purchase mutations were deleted.
- Shared shop stock preserves campaign rules for difficulty offer counts, Rich Man's Luck extras, character-exclusive moves, God Sweep unlocks, projectile-weapon representation and featured-god premium pricing.
- Enemy damage scaling is now one headless campaign operation in local play and authority: character/item/boss/bleed/bounty/challenge multipliers, elite and loop resistance, defense and flat reduction no longer have separate formulas.
- Enemy stat construction now also uses one shared campaign calculation for floor depth, loops, level, elapsed time, difficulty, boss/God modifiers and attack-speed growth. One network player is exact campaign parity; authority health scales linearly with the active party while damage and movement remain campaign values. Challenge, bounty, elite, split-minion and summoned-boss modifiers apply after that canonical calculation in campaign order.
- Crit chance, forced crits and rollback now resolve through one shared hit operation in local play and authority.
- Status storage/application, six-stack limits, duration/resistance math, player cold budgets, slow movement, enemy/player DoT formulas, bleed resistance and item/weapon on-hit proc rolls now resolve through `SharedStatusSystem` in both campaign and authority. Network presentation consumes the authority's real `statuses` map; legacy network bleed/fire/poison/freeze counters and reconstructed client statuses were deleted.
- Level-up XP growth, health/attack/attack-speed gains, Artificer bonuses and character milestones now resolve through one shared progression operation. Authority no longer overwrites a hero's character damage multiplier on level-up.
- Browser Playwright proves the normal campaign pause/settings flow, 2D/FPS/third-person rendering, two visible heroes, exact FPS beam yaw, Blade Justice, starter inventories, the normal item pickup card and clean server leave across two clients.
- Destructibles (pots, barrels, tables, walls) now break on the authority from melee sweeps, beams, AOE smashes and projectiles through one shared mutation, with the campaign break outcomes: pot coin/item loot, barrel 130-radius blast that chains to nearby props and damages enemies, and hidden-prop reveal on wall breaks. The client consumes `DESTRUCTIBLE_HIT`/`DESTRUCTIBLE_BROKEN` events to play the normal campaign hit/break/explosion FX and sounds.
- The local hero renders through the full campaign `drawPlayer` path in networked play. Remote allies use the same authored body, item scale, status/barrier/god/concealment alpha, hit flash, action frames, aim recoil and weapon animation through `drawPlayerSlot`; the only additions are player identity and down/revive UI.
- Campaign and multiplayer players share one 14 px physical radius. That radius drives authority collision, client prediction, 2D/3D sprite size, shadows, projectile origins and interaction reach; hibernated room checkpoints are normalized on wake so the removed 18 px multiplayer body cannot survive deployment.
- Third-person and first-person presentation use the same player alpha, action-facing, arm-recoil, downed pivot and melee-indicator implementation for the local hero and remote peers, including stab, Extending Staff and god/character styling.
- Pickup presentation is per-type like the campaign: the coin ring/chime only plays for coin pickups, potions show the authoritative heal popup, and item pickups show the normal card plus the compact "Copied!" toast on a duplicate roll.
- Encounter counts, seeded wave composition, ladder support units and floor-boss selection now come from `SharedEncounterSystem`; authority no longer maintains its fixed 1/2/3-enemy counts or a separate enemy pool.
- Projectile ricochet rolls, item speed/lifetime/pierce/homing modifiers, homing steering, movement integration, blocker reflection and perpendicular sub-spawn descriptors now come from `SharedProjectileSystem` in browser and authority.
- Configured weapon projectiles now use one shared factory in campaign and authority, including type/weapon/upgrade override precedence, merged hit effects, moving-gun recoil, and direct-type starter projectiles such as Sarge's Hammer.
- Primary attacks now share the campaign weapon cooldown/charge contract: charged weapons expose their authored pip counts, each spent pip reloads independently, the authority rejects exhausted presses, and clients suppress provisional attack/ability animation until the accepted action state is ready again.
- Projectile-to-destructible impacts now use a shared disposition: ordinary props take the real shot payload, Wall of Toph's friendly cover takes its authored one-chip self-contact, and Fire Balls retain their blocked-impact blast against enemies and nearby props.
- Hostile projectile blast payloads now share their validity, radius, damage, status and prop-damage policy. Multiplayer resolves those payloads when a shot expires or is blocked, rather than deleting area attacks at cover.
- Challenge bombs and explosive traps now use the shared campaign blast geometry for players, enemies and destructibles; authority no longer turns these authored room explosions into player-only damage.
- Sweepy Box now shares its armed thorn-mine policy (duration, arm/trigger radii, blast, knockback and bleed) in campaign and multiplayer. The authority also resolves persistent dungeon thorn mines from authored room templates against players, enemies and rock projectiles.
- El Barto's Cape now shares its deterministic Graffiti spawn plan and persistent raw-damage pulse profile; multiplayer snapshots the live zone rather than reducing the tool to its ambush flag.
- Wizard's Paw, Extra Battery, vouchers and all six control-scroll transactions (including pending queues, reroll, replace, abundance and pool weighting) now resolve through `SharedAcquisitionSystem` and validated game commands.
- Kill healing and kill-charge progression for Insurance, Keen Eye, Crit Charm, Chrono Spring, Charged Adapter, Robot Arm and Hemes Scarf now resolve through `SharedEventItemSystem` in both runtimes.
- Destructible HP/break state, secret-wall intent, hidden-group reveal, pot rewards, barrel blast intent and post-loop green drops now resolve through `SharedWorldMutationSystem`; local code retains only FX/audio and authority retains entity/event materialization.
- Titan Hammer now has one shared summon/motion policy. Multiplayer owns its live entity, cursor-follow transform, expiry, contact cadence, edge-triggered slams, stun/knockback and destructible damage; clients render that snapshot state instead of recreating a hammer from the cast event.

## Still blocking literal 1:1 parity

1. **Enemy behavior bodies**
   - The standard roster (hunter, charger, laser, knave, sniper, machine gunner, golem, cult mage/follower, summoner, shield unit, healer, boss spawner) now executes the authored campaign state machines on the authority via `SharedEnemyBehaviorSystem` — wind-ups, dashes, tracking beams, novas, bursts, cover-seeking, summons, shields, heals, the boss-spawner countdown, projectile evasion, elite traits (burning/bleeding/lazered) and hidden-player wander/blind-defense. Elite construction itself now has one `SharedEliteSystem` profile: stolen inventory, level-derived Knight/Knave/power tokens, durability, resistances, Giant/Lazered/Blessed state, elite crits and Enflamed/Gross/Breezy player-hit statuses all resolve from that policy on both runtimes. No walk-over contact damage; attacks deal all damage, like the campaign. Clients render the real telegraph fields (`windup`/`beamTime`/`beamAngle`/`dashTime`/`swingTime`) from snapshots.
   - The seven scripted bosses now also run their authored bodies on the authority: Cult Queen (missile volleys with dark-drain lifesteal, elite/golem summons, death-defying finisher with falloff blast), Bulk Golem (arcing jump-slam, nova cadence, golem kit and its scaled two-golem death split), Artificer (three phases: knave kit → converging phase swords → heavy swings), Bowman's Bane (phases, warp evasion, thunder smash, lightning columns and the Justice-of-Sonichu strike lines), Antony (bite drain, slash, directional hammer shockwave, cold death ball), Handsome Devil (red spikes, lava grid, twin lasers, claw) and the god's pattern table (laser/sweep/partition/charge/sword rings). Boss barks ride `ENEMY_SPOKE` events into the normal campaign speech bubbles; boss hazards live in authoritative room state and render through the campaign hazard renderer.
   - Mirror champions now snapshot the activator's live campaign kit and state on the authority (current HP, items, forge upgrades, inventory, equipped moves/weapons, derived combat stats and cooldowns), rather than spawning from a reduced default table. Their action selection is now one `SharedMirrorCombatSystem` policy in campaign and multiplayer (smash → laser → dash → weapon/melee and the same range/strafe posture); authority executes matching wind-ups, beams, dashes, melee and mirrored weapon projectiles rather than falling into the generic enemy loop. Mirrored Lazer Glases correctly enters the copied laser channel after its campaign weapon wind-up, rather than becoming a multiplayer-only bullet. Returning-rival placement now comes from the same scoped authority RNG used by other encounter entities, befriended rivals persist as non-hostile shadowing companions across floors, and `SharedRivalSystem` now drives the authored rival kit catalog/default-or-alternate slot selection, guarded warnings, opportunistic aggression, vendettas, recovery intent and the one-retreat-per-floor policy in both modes; authority performs the resulting doorway retreat as replicated state. Princess's rival Love Bomb now uses its shared full-size hostile projectile/detonation lifecycle; Rival Fire Staff now uses the same shared three-shot volley (full speed, recoil, direct burn and one-stack splash burn); Gelleh's Lightning Spear preserves the shared Smite blade geometry, pierce and static payload; Gelleh's low-health Healing Zone is now a shared persistent hostile zone that heals its owner and damages players at its campaign cadence; Turtle Boy's rival Death Ball uses the shared charged, piercing projectile plan; Metao's Potion Bath now shares its 20% self-heal, five-second protection window, and seven hostile bursts; and Gelleh's Holy Turrets and Excalibur Strike now share their clamped placement, authored timing, and hostile impact lifecycles. Rival Power Disks and Nail Shot now materialize their campaign shared projectile recipes (including disk shards and nail bounces), Random Pounce now produces its burst plus eight target-locked homing bleed fangs, and Blade Justice now owns the shared three-sword formation/motion/contact lifecycle in both modes, all from rival-scaled damage rather than degrading to generic mirror beams/smashes. Mooggy's rival Claw Gauntlets now also uses a shared two-swipe plan (delayed follow-up, range, damage, knockback and bleed), so authority keeps the campaign choreography even where the normal player-hit invulnerability blocks the damage component of the second swipe. Exact copied mirror kits now retain Nimrod Stomp, the campaign's short Flight and Coward's Way windows, Zip Lightning's 700-speed burst, Kicky Kick, Chaos Burst, Healing Zone, Fire Circle/Floor Lava, Power Disks, Blade Justice, Lightning Columns, the unarmed Fire Balls volley and the live Homing Missile smash proc. Their copied projectiles and direct hits inherit campaign crit, speed/bounce/homing/knockback and on-hit-status modifiers, rather than silently degrading those special dash/smash/laser/melee/projectile bodies to a generic mirror action; returning-rival-only Princess Shield, long Flight and Zoomies remain correctly scoped to rival actors. Lazered elites now produce the campaign's seeded, bounded two-column hazard pair through the authority hazard adapter. The god's rebirth phases 3–5 run from the shared behavior body, and its true death force-resolves every surviving council boss with campaign-equivalent ordinary rewards while suppressing their rebirth/finisher/split intercepts. Boss intro eligibility and dialogue content now resolve through `SharedBossIntroSystem`; authority persists and broadcasts the selected party-aware script, and the browser feeds it into the normal campaign dialogue UI. Remaining mirror-specific variants and the rival's other signature-move choreography bodies still need extraction. ## Deletion rule for remaining work

For each blocking row: extract the existing campaign mutation into a DOM-free operation; call it from local campaign and server authority; broadcast its state/events; let the normal browser renderer/UI consume them; delete the corresponding branch from `NetworkCombatSystem` or `NetworkGameView`.
